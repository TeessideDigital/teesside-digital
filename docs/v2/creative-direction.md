## 1. Creative north star

**APPROVED: Dark-default engineered editorial with a complementary light theme and purposeful kinetic, image-led interaction.**

**Engineered editorial + kinetic portfolio** combines three responsibilities:

- Engineered editorial supplies the vertical side-index, hierarchy, commercial clarity, useful explanation and evidence beside reasoning.
- Atmospheric dark materiality supplies tonal depth, controlled contrast and a distinctive slate/blue-charcoal environment.
- Kinetic portfolio interaction supplies responsive imagery, major visual moments, expressive links, spatial progression and deliberate movement.

The experience should feel high-end, atmospheric, cinematic, tactile, precise and visually memorable. It must also make sense to an ordinary small-business owner immediately. Cinematic means confident composition and pacing, not an introductory film or delayed access. Tactile means a response related to a visitor's action, not simulated machinery.

Open Edition remains the origin of the editorial structure, not a mandate for pale backgrounds or near-static presentation. A sidebar, a large heading and thin rules alone are insufficient. Distinction must come from the relationships between scale, material tone, imagery, spacing and interaction. The static and reduced-motion versions must still possess that identity.

Teesside Digital leads as the brand. Its audience remains small and owner-led businesses across sectors, initially Teesside, then the North East and suitable UK work. Services remain Websites & Web Development, Apps & Digital Tools, and Automation & AI. Security, accessibility and performance remain embedded standards. Visual confidence must not imply agency scale, enterprise-only work or specialist cybersecurity credentials.

## 2. Design principles

**APPROVED:** The following principles govern later decisions.

| Principle | What it requires | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Useful immediately | Establish the offer, orientation and access to enquiry without an introduction sequence. | Visitors should not have to decode a visual concept before recognising relevance. |
| Explain a real decision | Place evidence beside the reason, behaviour or limitation it supports. | Specific explanation lets a visitor assess judgement instead of accepting an adjective. |
| Structure before decoration | Use alignment, spacing and grouping to organise information. | An engineered character comes from coherent relationships, not engineering imagery. |
| Precision without clutter | Show metadata only when it helps interpret the evidence. | A date or method can establish context; ornamental labels merely imitate rigour. |
| Problem before technology | Begin with recognisable business difficulties, then introduce an appropriate response. | An owner can describe a problem without knowing an API, framework or model. |
| Brand before biography | Let the offer and work lead; use Dave's experience to establish responsibility. | The site represents a consultancy rather than a personal career portfolio. |
| Restraint with identity | Preserve distinctive composition even when motion and colour are removed. | Restraint must not become a generic minimal template. |
| Truth before completeness | Omit weak or unavailable proof rather than filling a reserved slot. | A visually complete page cannot justify a misleading impression. |

Problem-first communication is the only adopted communication principle from Connected Practice. Recognisable problem categories include missed enquiries, awkward quoting, repetitive administration, difficult booking, disconnected systems, an unclear website and slow manual processes. These are briefing categories, not final copy or claims that every visitor suffers from them.

Describe the problem, its relevant constraint and the possible response in plain language. Introduce technology only where it helps the visitor understand a decision. Do not import a task map, nodes, network graphics, branching homepage or diagram-led navigation. Do not imply that every customer needs all three services or that AI is necessary when ordinary automation is sufficient.

## 3. Emotional intent

**APPROVED:** The experience should develop informed confidence and earn attention through atmospheric, visibly crafted design. Visual impact and useful orientation must reinforce each other.

| Point in the visit | Intended feeling | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| First 5 seconds | Orientation and relevance | The business, service scope and next action are apparent; the opening feels purposeful. |
| After roughly 30 seconds | Confidence in the care and reasoning behind the work | Real evidence is accompanied by a concise, understandable explanation and honest limits. |
| At a decision to enquire | Comfort describing a situation directly to Dave | Contact feels accessible without requiring technical self-diagnosis or a compulsory call. |

These are design intentions, not measured visitor responses. Avoid exclusivity, academic distance, bureaucratic density and an impression that only sophisticated technology buyers belong here. High quality must not be signalled through unnecessary expense, inflated seniority or implied agency scale.

## 4. Visual narrative

**APPROVED:** The experience moves from understanding the offer to inspecting relevant evidence, recognising responsibility and making an informed enquiry. This is a narrative principle, not a prescribed section sequence.

The opening establishes the brand and services within an asymmetric reading structure. The side index provides a stable alternative to reading linearly. Further content varies in density: compact factual groups, a larger piece of evidence where it merits attention, and concise explanation beside it. Whitespace marks changes in subject and importance rather than repeatedly filling a screen.

The enquiry route remains available throughout the experience; visitors are not required to reach the final section. A closing invitation should resolve uncertainty about who receives the enquiry and what kind of information is useful. A manual website review is secondary and must remain recognisable as subsequent work performed by Dave.

No automated Website Health Check, percentage score or simulated report-generation sequence returns. The approved content system and experience architecture govern page order, destinations and conversion. Eligible evidence can create large image-led moments and spatial progression; without it, the approved homepage sequence remains complete, with no reserved blank proof region.

## 5. Typography philosophy

**APPROVED:** Use a strong editorial display voice alongside a highly readable body and interface system. The contrast between expressive and functional text must establish hierarchy and support reading. It must not be a decorative pairing chosen simply to look sophisticated.

Display text carries important statements without relying on extreme size, thin strokes, clipped words or theatrical line breaks. Body text, navigation, labels and captions remain comfortable to read. Metadata is functional text with clear labels; it does not become tiny all-caps ornament or monospace used to signal technology.

**PROVISIONAL:** Source Serif 4 and Source Sans 3 remain candidates from the options document. Neither family is approved for production. The precise serif/sans solution, number of families, weights, numerical styling, fallbacks and font-loading approach remain unresolved. No third technical font is justified merely by the engineered character.

**PROVISIONAL:** Final type scale, line-height, tracking, line lengths and responsive interpolation. The options document's sample sizes and weights are not binding tokens. Do not harden those values into a design system before approved content exists.

Later evaluation must consider:

- Fit with Teesside Digital's practical, technical and visually confident identity, without luxury-publication associations.
- Distinction from the actual North East Joinery typography and hierarchy, not just a different font name.
- Real approved headings, service names, navigation labels, long words, captions and form messages.
- Reading comfort, character clarity, text enlargement, wrapping, spacing overrides and narrow-screen behaviour.
- Actual font files, licence terms, required styles, transfer cost and fallback layout stability.

The roles are approved; the families must earn selection through that evaluation. A typeface's reputation or popularity does not establish its suitability.

## 6. Colour philosophy and theme behaviour

**APPROVED:** Dark is the first-visit art direction. Use deep slate, blue-charcoal, dark mineral and ink-blue relationships. A dark main field and slightly lighter secondary planes create material depth; soft off-white primary text and cool grey secondary text establish reading hierarchy. Do not define the atmosphere through pure black, neon or blue glows.

Retain/evolve the existing steel-blue identity, including the recognisable relationship to `#5b7f9e`. Develop a stronger functional blue for appropriate interactive roles. The existing brand value is an asset reference, not approval that it passes every contrast pairing. No other exact colour values are settled here.

**APPROVED:** Light is a deliberately designed alternative using cool mineral grey, soft off-white, dark slate typography and restrained steel blue. It preserves composition, hierarchy, typography roles, imagery, motion and interaction language. It is not stark white, a mechanical inversion or a second creative territory.

| Situation | Required product behaviour |
|---|---|
| First visit, no explicit preference | Dark, irrespective of the operating system's theme preference |
| Visitor selects Dark or Light | Apply that choice and meaningfully communicate the selected state |
| Later visit with a valid remembered choice | Honour the explicit choice before the default |
| Persistence unavailable or invalid | Recommended safe fallback: retain a usable dark default; allow a choice for the current visit where enhancement works |
| Enhancement unavailable | Essential content, links and enquiry remain fully usable in the default theme |

The quiet Dark / Light utility belongs to the side-index system, including its compact transformation. Do not create a prominent Dark / Light / System control. It needs an understandable accessible name, meaningful selected state, visible focus and keyboard operation. An icon or colour alone is insufficient.

Later implementation must avoid an obvious light-to-dark flash and honour remembered light without an obvious incorrect-theme flash. Exact storage, persistence lifetime, initialisation and control semantics remain DEFERRED. Failure to save a preference must not block navigation or content. Do not promise persistence if storage has failed.

Final surface/text/rule/focus/hover/pressed/status values are DEFERRED. Test each theme independently, including link states, control boundaries and form feedback. Do not infer light-theme contrast from dark-theme results. Status uses text as well as colour; services do not acquire separate bright brand palettes.

## 7. Grid and composition

**APPROVED:** A desktop side index establishes orientation beside an asymmetric editorial content field. Main content, evidence and explanation share deliberate alignment relationships. The composition should feel ordered without making every region equal.

Use a stable main reading edge and a supporting evidence/annotation edge where space allows. The amount of space assigned to a piece of evidence follows what the visitor needs to inspect. Related notes align to their subject, not to arbitrary coordinates. Technical precision appears through consistent spacing relationships, clear grouping and meaningful rules.

Density must vary with purpose. A short service overview can be compact; a detailed screenshot may need breadth; an explanatory passage needs a comfortable reading width. Major topic gaps should be visibly different from spacing within a related group. Do not use identical large vertical padding for every section.

**PROVISIONAL:** Exact column count, spans, side-index width, gutters, spacing increments and maximum content width. The earlier 12-column examples are possible working models, not approved geometry. Logical reading and keyboard order must remain valid before visual placement is applied.

Avoid arbitrary bento arrangements, repeated equal service cards, decorative background grids and bespoke positional exceptions for every page. A reusable set of relationships is more precise and maintainable than individually nudged elements. At large widths, preserve useful reading measures rather than stretching text indefinitely.

## 9. Material and visual language

**APPROVED:** Material tone and layered tonal depth establish the dark atmosphere. Use coherent opaque planes, controlled contrast, generous image fields where evidence qualifies, precise rules and purposeful whitespace. Depth need not mean raised containers or a shadow around every group.

A secondary plane may distinguish a substantial reading or evidence field; it must not turn each service into a filled card or the index into application chrome. Light mode translates these relationships into mineral-grey and soft off-white tones while preserving the same hierarchy.

Rules organise real groups. Annotation identifies a feature, behaviour, decision or limitation. Metadata must be useful and verifiable. Proximity normally connects explanation and evidence more clearly than ornamental lines.

Explicitly reject glowing purple/blue blobs, glass cards, neon gradient text, fake terminals, circuit graphics, hacker visuals, blue glow on black, floating laptops, chrome 3D objects, starfields, particles and generic AI-company aesthetics. Also avoid aged paper, fashion imagery, artificial grain and decorative code. Dark is not permission to recover V1's decorative technology vocabulary.

Real screenshots retain context and truthful status. Crops can be cinematic and large, but must not conceal a limitation or fabricate functionality. Keep captions outside imagery where needed for contrast and reading. Exact radii, depth treatments, line weights and cropping details remain PROVISIONAL.

## 10. Hero philosophy

It must establish what Teesside Digital does, help the visitor recognise relevance and preserve direct access to enquiry. The side index participates in orientation. Evidence may be included only where a suitable real item strengthens understanding; the composition must also work honestly without prominent proof.

Do not revert to a large centred headline, supporting paragraph, two CTA buttons, large decorative visual and excessive empty space. Replacing the colours and type in that arrangement would not implement Open Edition.

Compact does not mean cramped or forced above the fold. Enlarged text, long approved service names and narrow viewports must be able to increase the opening height naturally. Service-led and problem-first are compatible: describe relevance in business language while making the service scope clear.

**Phase 4 revised recommendation:** Option A - Offer and directory remains preferred structurally: unequal offer/directory fields, open service list and enquiry in the index. Revise its art direction through dark material tone, stronger scale relationships, tonal depth and purposeful interaction. The hero remains usable without imagery; this is not a text-only mandate for the rest of the website. ## 11. Section composition language

**APPROVED:** Use a small, coherent vocabulary of reading and evidence relationships rather than one repeated card-and-heading arrangement.

| Relationship | Appropriate purpose | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Compact information group | Help visitors understand service scope or compare relevant facts | Keep labels plain; do not create a dense specification sheet for its own sake. |
| Evidence beside explanation | Show what was implemented and why it matters | Notes must relate to the actual evidence and remain readable. |
| Wider evidence with a nearby detail | Let a visitor inspect something a small image cannot communicate | Use only where real proof merits the space; preserve context. |
| Focused reading passage | Explain a decision, responsibility or limitation | Control length and measure; avoid an uninterrupted essay. |
| Direct task area | Let someone enquire or request a manual review | Keep instructions and feedback clear; do not insert a promotional obstacle. |

These are compositional relationships, not a final section list or component API. Consistent alignment and spacing join them into one site. Not every section needs an annotation, index number, border or distinct surface.

Service, work, founder, contact and legal content can share the same design language while meeting different reading needs. A contact page should not inherit a tall marketing hero merely for visual consistency. The approved Phase 3 routes, section responsibilities, content allocation and CTA progression govern; this visual amendment does not reopen them.

## 12. Work and evidence presentation

**APPROVED - core signature:** Real evidence and adjacent explanation form the defining visual grammar. Each annotation must improve understanding of something that exists or has genuinely been measured.

| Evidence | Required explanatory relationship |
|---|---|
| Actual screenshot | Identify the relevant design decision and why it was made. |
| Actual interaction | Explain behaviour, including important failure or recovery conditions where relevant. |
| Measured result | State what was measured, date, environment, method and limitations. |
| Project status | Make the current state and material constraints visible. |
| Implemented feature | Explain the business purpose without inventing a commercial result. |

The explanation should distinguish fact, interpretation and limitation. A feature being implemented is not proof of more sales; a fast local measurement is not a universal performance guarantee. Do not inflate a small observation into a complete accessibility or security assessment.

- **SiteGuard:** Exclude from current public proof. This does not permanently delete the idea.
- **Teesside Digital V2:** Its eventual implementation can demonstrate design and engineering care, supported by real evidence. It cannot substitute for client outcomes or an established specialist consultancy record.

Status must agree across summaries, detail pages, captions, links and any emitted metadata. Avoid empty project slots, invented timelines, client-logo treatment for fictional businesses and unsupported results. A smaller truthful proof area is acceptable.

Once evidence qualifies, imagery can become a major part of the website: large cinematic crops, edge-reaching fields, varied aspect ratios, asymmetric placement, sequences, detail crops, controlled overflow and title/image relationships. ## 14. Motion philosophy

**APPROVED: Purposeful kinetic interaction is part of the Teesside Digital brand experience.** The former ultra-restrained interpretation is superseded. Motion may contribute visual character as well as feedback, provided it remains related to content, orientation or visitor action.

| Territory for Phase 5 exploration | Purpose | Required equivalent/limit |
|---|---|---|
| Service text, rule, arrow and descriptor response | Make a useful entry feel responsive and deliberate | Stable hit area, full label and description available at rest |
| Controlled neighbouring-item de-emphasis | Clarify which service is being considered | Neighbours remain readable, discoverable and usable |
| Image scale, crop movement and mask/clip reveal | Direct attention to a meaningful visual detail | Real evidence and explanatory text available without the effect |
| Project changes and title/image relationships | Preserve orientation between a project and its views | Labelled direct access; no hover-only discovery |
| Subtle pointer response within an image | Add controlled physical response where appropriate | Optional on fine pointers, unnecessary on touch/keyboard |
| Moving current-section indicator | Clarify a real navigation state change | Correct static indication with reduced motion |
| Image-led progression, density changes and responsive recomposition | Create purposeful pacing as space/content changes | Logical order, stable access and complete static presentation |

These are permitted directions, not a list of effects to install. Prioritise a few coherent behaviours. All essential content and actions must be usable immediately; never hold the opening behind an animation or make forms appear successful before acceptance.

Still prohibited: scroll-jacking, forced horizontal scrolling, preloaders, long introductions, animation before content is usable, constant floating objects, universal fade-up, bouncing scroll indicators, cursor replacement, gratuitous WebGL, endless marquees, particle backgrounds, excessive parallax and decorative video. Meaning must never depend on animation.

Reduced motion is a complete presentation with the same information, orientation and actions. Replace spatial travel, crop movement and reveal sequences with static states or direct changes; do not merely shorten an otherwise disorienting sequence.

**DEFERRED to Phase 5:** Exact choreography, triggers, timings, easing, transition coordination, interruptions and device-specific behaviour. None is specified or implemented here.

## 15. Interaction philosophy

**APPROVED:** Expressive responses sit on dependable, familiar controls. Stable targets and visible affordance make kinetic interaction usable.

- Links navigate; buttons act. Controls are recognisable without hover.
- A link's inner text, arrow or rule may move subtly in later exploration while its hit area and focus reference stay stable. Do not move the actionable target away from the pointer or hide the label.
- Keyboard focus receives equivalent clarity and content; it need not reproduce pointer tracking. Touch needs direct activation without a preliminary hover step.
- Keep the native cursor. Optional subtle image response is permitted; cursor replacement, magnetic targets and unrelated pointer-following decoration are not.
- Essential evidence, descriptors, status and limitations stay available at rest. No hotspot, reveal or animation is a prerequisite to understand them.
- Theme choice uses a named control with an understandable state, visible focus and robust fallback.
- Forms distinguish sending, known acceptance and failure. Errors identify recovery steps and preserve entered information where possible.

The primary enquiry asks about the business problem without requiring technical diagnosis. The manual review remains separate: Dave reviews the public site, prepares a structured report and emails it personally. No instant analysis, invented completion or fixed turnaround is implied.

Approved Phase 2/3 form content and task structure remain unchanged. Detailed interaction styling, theme-control construction and motion behaviour are DEFERRED. This amendment authorises no component changes.

## 16. Mobile transformation

**APPROVED:** Mobile transforms Open Edition's relationships deliberately. It must not be a miniature desktop spread or a mechanically stacked sequence that separates evidence from its explanation.

| Desktop relationship | Smaller-screen transformation |
|---|---|
| Side index beside the content | Compact brand-and-directory arrangement with clear orientation, direct enquiry access and a quiet usable theme control. |
| Evidence with a marginal note | A useful evidence extract immediately followed by its explanation. |
| Unequal reading and evidence columns | A coherent reading axis with selective variation in image width and caption treatment. |
| Broad screenshot | A relevant readable view with access to the wider context, rather than illegible reduction. |
| Varied section density | Tighter grouping of related information, retaining clear transitions between topics. |

Prefer visible, wrapping navigation where it remains practical. Exact arrangement and any disclosure mechanism remain provisional, but navigation must remain usable when enhancement fails. Avoid hidden sideways navigation or a persistent overlay that covers content and form fields.

On tablets, preserve multiple columns only while both remain useful. On short landscape viewports and at high zoom, release sticky behaviour where it obstructs access. Do not force headings or service labels onto one line by shrinking them. Keep the service scope intact across sizes.

Both themes retain the same meaningful content and hierarchy on mobile. Image movement must not require hover or obscure reading; reduced motion and touch receive complete static access. Preserve deliberate image scale and context rather than shrinking a desktop crop until it is illegible.

**PROVISIONAL:** Exact breakpoints, rail-to-directory threshold, widths, cropping rules and responsive type values. The project's named viewport sizes are later test conditions, not automatically the CSS breakpoints. Accessibility and content pressure determine the transitions.

## 17. Accessibility implications

**APPROVED:** Target WCAG 2.2 AA and preserve the ability to read, navigate, inspect evidence and enquire without unnecessary interaction or motion. This is a target, not a claim of tested conformance.

The direction's strengths are visible orientation, stable reading, clear grouping and an immediately available document. Its risks are narrow annotations, overly dense information, expressive display text, side-index obstruction and a visual sequence that differs from source order.

Later design and implementation must address those risks:

- Keep captions and metadata readable; do not use miniature text to make the composition appear technical.
- Preserve logical headings, landmarks, source order and keyboard sequence when applying asymmetric layouts.
- Verify focus visibility, target usability and unobstructed access with the side index, including short viewports and zoom.
- Test all colour states and surfaces in both themes, including de-emphasised neighbours, controls and focus, not just the primary text pair. Important meaning cannot depend on colour alone.
- Keep explanations available as text. An annotated screenshot does not replace accessible information or prove the depicted interface is accessible.
- Ensure long labels and URLs reflow, and support text enlargement and spacing adjustments without lost content.
- Provide meaningful error associations and recovery, and ensure reduced motion preserves the same information and actions.

**DEFERRED:** Detailed accessibility specifications and testing records for the chosen layouts and controls. If testing exposes a conflict, correct the layout or behaviour and document any proposed change to an approved signature. Do not claim a pass because the direction appears restrained.

## 19. Commercial rationale

**APPROVED:** The primary commercial outcome is a qualified project enquiry sent directly to Dave. The manual website review remains secondary. The site should help ordinary owners assess relevance and trust without demanding technical knowledge.

Open Edition supports that outcome through a visible service offer, clear navigation and specific evidence. It can present a small amount of honest work coherently now, then accommodate genuine client cases later. It does not require a mature agency portfolio, fabricated social proof or an ongoing publishing programme.

The engineered amendment strengthens this rationale: precise status, behaviour and limitations help visitors distinguish evidence from promotion. Problem-first communication connects that evidence to recognisable needs across sectors. It must not narrow the market back to trades or imply that a website customer must also buy apps and automation.

The main commercial risks remain excessive reading, a literary or luxury tone, inaccessible technical density and generic editorial styling. Control these through useful hierarchy and plain language. Do not equate expensive appearance with customer value, invent a new price range or position solo delivery as simply cheaper than an agency.

The website can demonstrate care through its own behaviour. It cannot, by design alone, establish client outcomes, broad cybersecurity competence or unlimited delivery capacity. Detailed qualification, pricing, support boundaries and final persuasion copy remain outside this phase.

## 21. Failure modes

**APPROVED guardrails:** Later work must actively avoid these degraded interpretations.

| Failure mode | Recognisable symptom | Correction within the approved direction |
|---|---|---|
| Luxury publication | Delicate serif, fashion-like empty space and little visible service relevance | Restore practical information, functional typography and real evidence; re-evaluate provisional fonts. |
| V1 in editorial clothing | Centred slogan, paragraph, paired CTAs and three cards in either theme | Rebuild the opening and section relationships around the side index and asymmetric reading structure. |
| Decorative engineering | Fake schematics, code fragments, calibration marks or meaningless metadata | Remove the decoration; show only useful structure and verifiable annotations. |
| Technical bureaucracy | Tiny captions, excessive rules and a wall of specifications | Reduce secondary detail, strengthen hierarchy and explain the business purpose first. |
| Token approval by accident | Proposal fonts, hex values or timings treated as fixed implementation instructions | Return them to PROVISIONAL status and evaluate them against real content and constraints. |
| Side-index erosion | Conventional desktop top navigation substituted because it is easier to build | Restore the approved principle; escalate only evidenced usability/accessibility conflicts for review. |
| Proof inflation | Polished framing implies finished client work or verified outcomes that do not exist | Correct status and limitations, reduce prominence or omit the item. |
| Annotation as ornament | Notes restate marketing claims without explaining a feature or observation | Link each explanation to real evidence, or remove it. |
| Territory copying | Green task maps, copied portfolio choreography, bottom navigation or imported exhibition branding | Restore Teesside Digital's side-index, material tone and evidence relationships; interpret reference qualities independently. |
| Mechanical mobile stacking | Captions detach from evidence, screenshots become illegible and navigation is hidden | Recompose the reading sequence and directory for the available space. |
| Motion dependence | Content appears only after scrolling, or the page loses identity without animation | Make content available immediately and restore structure as the source of distinction. |
| Fragile precision | Manual line breaks and positional fixes break when text or viewport changes | Use robust relationships and test real content; avoid screenshot-specific layout assumptions. |

Additional amendment-specific failures are a light-first default, a pure-black SaaS treatment, a token-inverted light theme, a prominent System selector, full-logo compression in the rail, decorative motion everywhere, or a text-only website despite qualified visual evidence. Correct these through the approved dark/light product rules, mark-plus-live-text identity, focused kinetic behaviours and truthful image-led composition. Visual restraint that reproduces the rejected generic mock-up also fails.
